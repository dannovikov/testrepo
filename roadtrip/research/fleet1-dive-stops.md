## ANCHOR 1 — Whitewater Falls (~10:10am, 15 min)
- **Google Maps:** "Whitewater Falls" (Sapphire, NC / NC-281)
- **GPS:** 35.0296, -83.0159 (parking lot)
- **What you see:** 411-ft Upper Whitewater Falls — tallest waterfall east of the Rockies — thundering down a green gorge wall.
- **Walk:** 0.2 mi paved, gently uphill, ~4–5 min to the upper overlook. There are 154 wooden steps down to a lower platform — **skip them**, the upper rail view is the money shot.
- **Parking:** Large paved USFS lot, pull-through-easy for the 7 Series. No restrooms/water per current USFS page.
- **Monday-July crowd at 10am:** Light — USFS warns about midday/holiday crush; you're there at opening lull the morning after the holiday. Expect 2–5 cars.
- **Photo:** From the paved upper overlook rail, shoot slightly down-gorge with the full 411-ft drop framed by ridgelines; morning side-light, bring/use polarizer mode. White car won't be in frame — falls face away from the lot.
- **Fee:** $3/vehicle self-pay envelope or recreation.gov. **Tip:** the $15 annual pass covers Whitewater + Dry Falls + Whiteside — but two $3 envelopes is cheaper for one day.

## BONUS 1a — Bridal Veil Falls (~11:10am, 5 min)
- **Google Maps:** "Bridal Veil Falls" (US-64, Highlands, NC)
- **GPS:** 35.0757, -83.2325
- **What you see:** 45-ft ribbon falls pouring over a rock arch of old US-64 right at the road edge.
- **IMPORTANT expectation reset:** you can **no longer drive the car behind it** — the old drive-under loop is permanently barricaded (boulder fall history); it's a 30-second stroll behind the curtain instead. Verified via [WNC Waterfalls](https://www.wncwaterfalls.info/waterfall/7/Bridal_Veil_Falls).
- **Walk:** 30 seconds. This is the day's lowest-effort waterfall.
- **Parking:** Paved pull-off apron directly in front of the arch, room for 3–4 cars parallel/angled; trivially easy, fully off the highway.
- **Crowd:** Light — people cycle through in 5 minutes.
- **Photo:** Stand behind the falls under the arch shooting outward through the water toward US-64 — or across the road for the classic postcard with the white 7 Series parked under the ledge-adjacent apron (the one legal "car + waterfall" shot of the day).
- **Fee:** Free.

## ANCHOR 2 — Dry Falls, the walk-BEHIND waterfall (~11:20–11:45am, 20 min)
- **Google Maps:** "Dry Falls" (US-64, Highlands, NC)
- **GPS:** 35.0681, -83.2387 (lot is 0.6 mi past Bridal Veil)
- **What you see:** 75-ft curtain falls on the Cullasaja River that the walkway passes **completely behind** — you stand in a dry rock alcove with the river exploding over your head. This is the route's required "walk behind a waterfall" stop, and July flow is ideal (impressive but not soaking the path).
- **Walk:** ~5 min down: paved path then stone/concrete steps with handrails, ~0.2 mi round trip. Short but real stairs — still firmly inside the no-hike rule.
- **Parking:** Paved USFS lot (~20 spaces) with marked bays; easy for the sedan **before noon**. This is the one lot on the route that genuinely crushes midday — your 11:20 arrival on a post-holiday Monday is the play. Flush restrooms at the lot.
- **Monday-July crowd at 11:30:** Moderate — busiest stop of your day, but Monday-after-July-4th takes the top off. After 12:30 it becomes a circling-for-spaces situation; don't slip later.
- **Photo:** From inside the alcove behind the curtain, shoot downstream through the falling water with the gorge lit beyond (phone in one hand, mist wipe in the other). Second angle: the side profile from the last stair landing.
- **Fee:** $3/vehicle.

## BONUS 2a — Cullasaja Falls pull-off (~12:00pm, 3 min, ONLY IF CLEAR)
- **Google Maps:** "Cullasaja Falls" (US-64, ~9760 Highlands Rd)
- **GPS:** 35.1119, -83.2915
- **What you see:** 250-ft staircase cascade filling the gorge — biggest waterfall on the Byway, viewed from the car window.
- **Walk:** Zero — it's a windshield/shoulder view.
- **Parking:** The day's **worst** parking: a short, narrow shoulder pull-off on a blind-ish curve, ~2–3 cars. A 7 Series fits but with tail near the white line. **Rule: if a car is already there or you'd have to brake late, skip it** — you've already banked three waterfalls.
- **Crowd:** Light (because the pull-off is scary).
- **Photo:** From the pull-off looking back upstream; go wide/vertical to stack the full cascade.
- **Fee:** Free.

*(12:45–1:30 — Bryson City fuel + quick lunch. Non-negotiable fuel stop: nothing real between Stecoah and Tellico Plains.)*

## ANCHOR 3 — Road to Nowhere + tunnel (~1:40–2:40pm, the signature stop)
- **Google Maps:** "Road to Nowhere Tunnel" or "Lakeview Drive East, Bryson City" (drive to the literal end)
- **GPS:** tunnel parking ~35.4605, -83.5375
- **What you see:** 6.5 miles of **2025-fresh, glass-smooth NPS pavement** sweeping into Great Smoky Mountains NP, big Fontana Lake overlook pull-offs on the left around miles 4–6 (signed, obvious, usually empty — stop at the best one), then a dead-end at a barricade and a 365-yard abandoned 1970s tunnel you walk through. Turn-off-the-main-road, dead-end, nobody-there — the platonic version of the brief.
- **Walk:** ~150 yards / 2–3 min from the turnaround lot to the tunnel mouth; walking the tunnel itself is optional (~10 min out-and-back, bring phone flashlight; floor is paved and flat).
- **Parking:** Paved turnaround/lot with new ADA spaces from the 2025 rebuild — effortless. **Display the $5 GSMNP parking tag** (stop exceeds 15 min).
- **Monday-July crowd:** Famously light; on a Monday afternoon expect 0–4 other vehicles at the tunnel and empty overlooks. No cell service — offline maps loaded.
- **Photo:** Dead-center of the road shooting straight into the tunnel bore (symmetry + graffiti glow); and the white sedan alone on fresh blacktop with Fontana Lake behind at the mile-5 overlook — likely the day's best car shot.
- **Fee:** Park entry free; $5 parking tag (also covers Look Rock tonight).
- **Status:** OPEN, reconstruction fully complete (reopened Aug 2025; nothing on NPS closure page dated 6/30/2026).

## BONUS 3a — Cable Cove Boat Ramp, the deserted-dock encore (~3:10pm, 10–15 min)
- **Google Maps:** "Cable Cove Boat Ramp" (via Cable Cove Rd/SR-1287 off NC-28, 8.2 mi past the NC-143 turnoff)
- **GPS:** ~35.4300, -83.7660 (ramp at road's end)
- **What you see:** A lonely concrete ramp and cove on Fontana Lake ringed by national-forest ridges — the Lake Hartwell dock moment, replayed. Sit on the hood, engine ticking, nobody around.
- **Walk:** Zero — park at the water.
- **Parking:** Gravel but short (~1.4 mi), graded and sedan-safe at 15 mph; ramp lot is flat. Fine for the 7 Series if driven gently; skip only if it's been storming hard.
- **Monday-July crowd:** A fisherman's truck-and-trailer or two at most on a post-holiday Monday; often literally empty.
- **Photo:** From the ramp toe, low angle across the water up the cove; late-afternoon light comes down the lake.
- **Fee:** Free.

## ANCHOR 4 — Fontana Dam (~3:30–4:00pm, 20 min)
- **Google Maps:** "Fontana Dam Visitor Center"
- **GPS:** 35.4518, -83.8046
- **What you see:** 480-ft Fontana Dam — tallest dam east of the Rockies — with Fontana Lake on one side and the Little Tennessee gorge on the other; the Appalachian Trail crosses the top.
- **Walk:** 1 min from lot to the observation deck; walking/driving across the dam crest is optional (+10 min).
- **Parking:** Free paved lots at the visitor center and across the dam; easy.
- **Crowd:** Light — a handful of AT hikers and dam nerds.
- **Photo:** From the visitor-center overlook deck shooting along the dam's curve with the lake beyond; the downstream face from the crest is the vertigo shot.
- **Fee:** Free. Visitor center open 9–7 (Mon Jul 6 is not a federal holiday — it's open).

## BONUS 4a — Cheoah Dam, "The Fugitive" jump (~4:15pm, 5 min)
- **Google Maps:** "Cheoah Dam" (US-129 at Tapoco)
- **GPS:** overlook 35.4519, -83.9385, 1.8 mi south of the US-129/NC-28 junction
- **What you see:** The 1919 dam Harrison Ford swan-dived off in The Fugitive, wedged into a gorge; Cheoah Lake pull-offs on the way in are reliable empty "smoke spot" water views.
- **Walk:** Zero to 1 min (view is through/over a chain-link fence at the US-129 overlook; a cleaner angle is from the NC-28 bridge pull-off just north).
- **Parking:** Small paved overlook pull-off; fine for the sedan. Watch for sport bikes — this is the Dragon's southern approach and post-holiday Monday still has bike spillover on US-129 here. It's your only bike-traffic exposure of the day and you exit it immediately via NC-143.
- **Crowd:** Light-moderate (bikers pausing).
- **Photo:** Through the fence gap head-on to the spillway face; movie-recreation vertical.
- **Fee:** Free.

## ANCHOR 5 — Cherohala Skyway / Santeetlah Overlook (~4:30–6:00pm)
- **Google Maps:** "Santeetlah Overlook" (Cherohala Skyway, mile 11 NC side, at the crest near the NC/TN line)
- **GPS:** ~35.348, -84.013 (pin is accurate in Google Maps under the name)
- **What you see:** The highest point of the Skyway at **5,390 ft** — layered blue ridges over the Santeetlah Creek watershed, Joyce Kilmer–Slickrock Wilderness and Snowbird Mountains, frequently above cloud shreds after afternoon storms.
- **Walk:** Steps from the car; picnic tables at the rail.
- **Parking:** Big paved crescent pull-off, angle-in, effortless. Every Skyway overlook is like this — 43 miles of flawless pavement with a dozen paved, usually-empty overlooks (Spirit Ridge has a 0.25-mi paved accessible path if you want one more; Big Junction and Turkey Creek on the TN side are pure drive-ups).
- **Monday-July crowd at 5pm:** Near-empty — a couple of motorcycles at most; weekday evenings the Skyway is deserted.
- **Photo:** Golden-hour side light on the ridge stacks looking southwest; car-on-empty-overlook wide shot is easy here — nobody to wait out.
- **Fee:** Free, no tag needed (USFS byway).
- **Weather rule (unchanged from judge's notes):** check radar at Robbinsville ~4:15; if cells are building, run the Skyway non-stop and take the view budget to Bald River Falls instead.

## ANCHOR 6 (route-designated "optional +30," recommend KEEP) — Bald River Falls (~6:15pm, 15 min)
- **Google Maps:** "Bald River Falls" (Tellico River Rd/FS-210, 6 mi off the Skyway from Tellico Plains side)
- **GPS:** 35.3208, -84.1740
- **What you see:** A 90-ft waterfall crashing directly beside the road — viewable **from the driver's seat on the bridge**, and since the May 30, 2025 rebuild the new two-lane bridge has a dedicated **observation deck**, which the old bridge never had. Best flow-to-effort ratio of the entire day.
- **Walk:** Zero from the bridge deck; ~1 min from the small paved pull-in just past the bridge.
- **Parking:** Small paved lot/pull-offs at the falls; fine for the 7 Series on a weekday evening (weekend middays it overflows). FS-210 to the falls is paved the whole way.
- **Monday-July crowd at 6:15pm:** Light — families gone home; maybe a couple of swimmers below the falls.
- **Photo:** From the new observation deck on the bridge, wide vertical of the full 90-ft face; evening shade makes the water silky even handheld.
- **Fee:** Free.
- **Status:** OPEN — verified reopened May 30, 2025 after the 2-year bridge/road rebuild ([WVLT](https://www.wvlt.tv/2025/05/29/bald-river-falls-bridge-reopen-after-years-long-overhaul/)); no 2026 closure alerts on the Cherokee NF pages.

## ANCHOR 7 — Look Rock Overlook, Foothills Parkway (~7:15–7:40pm, sunset staging)
- **Google Maps:** "Look Rock Overlook, Foothills Parkway" (after the Chilhowee Lake run up US-129 — grab one lakeside pull-off on Chilhowee if the light is good, 35.556, -84.010 area)
- **GPS:** 35.6338, -83.9418
- **What you see:** 2,600-ft westward sweep over the Tennessee Valley and Chilhowee Mountain from a drive-up stone-wall overlook; the Smokies crest behind you. At 7:15pm in July the valley is going gold — this is your sunset-adjacent finale with ~90 min of light still banked for the run to Alcoa.
- **Walk:** Zero for the overlook wall. The observation tower is a 0.5-mi paved climb — **skip**; the wall gives 85% of the view for 0% of the walk (tower confirmed open if you ever return).
- **Parking:** Long paved parallel overlook aprons on the Parkway; trivial. **Keep the $5 GSMNP tag displayed** — Foothills Parkway is inside park boundaries and >15 min requires it.
- **Monday-July crowd at 7:15pm:** Light — a few sunset watchers; this section never carries Gatlinburg traffic.
- **Photo:** West-southwest into the haze-layered valley with the stone wall as foreground leading line; if the car shot matters, the parallel apron puts the 7 Series broadside to the view.
- **Fee:** $5 parking tag (already bought this morning — same tag as Road to Nowhere).

Whitewater 10:10 → Dry Falls 11:20 → Road to Nowhere 1:40 → Fontana Dam 3:30 → Santeetlah 5:00 → Bald River 6:15 → Look Rock 7:15.
