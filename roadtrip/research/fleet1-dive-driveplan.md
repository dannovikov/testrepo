## 2. FUEL PLAN (BMW needs 91+; Exxon/Shell in the Carolinas carry 93)

Total route ≈ 305–320 mi. Two mandatory fills, one insurance top-off:

1. **9:20am — Seneca, SC (mile 0):** Top off on Sandifer Blvd/US-123 (multiple 93-octane stations — e.g., *Ingles Gas Express, Seneca SC*, 3 min from the hotel). SC fuel is the cheapest of the day. Leave FULL.
2. **12:30pm — Cullasaja Exxon, 6241 Highlands Rd (US-64), Franklin NC** (Google: "Cullasaja Exxon Franklin NC"). Sits at the exact bottom of the gorge descent — zero detour. 93 octane. **This is the anchor fill: after Bryson City there is NO gas on Lakeview Dr, NC-28 past Stecoah, or anywhere near Fontana.** Backup in Bryson City: *Time Out Markets 23 (Exxon), 3091 US-19 S, Bryson City*.
3. **~6:00pm — Shell, 374 Rodney Orr Bypass, Robbinsville NC** (verified Shell locator). **Last fuel for 50+ mi** — nothing on the 43-mi Cherohala until Tellico Plains. Even if the tank reads half, put 93 in; you don't want to be hunting premium in Tellico Plains at 8pm (its two stations at the TN-165/68 junction don't reliably carry 93).

Gas deserts: Franklin→Robbinsville ≈ 90 route-miles between fills (incl. the Road to Nowhere out-and-back); Robbinsville→Tellico Plains 50 mi with nothing; Tellico Plains→Maryville sparse until Vonore.

## 3. CELL COVERAGE — WHERE IT DIES (download offline maps for the whole corridor before leaving Seneca)

- **SC-130/NC-281 gorge:** spotty from Salem SC to the falls; dead at the Whitewater lot (Forest Service itself warns "connectivity is limited" at the fee site).
- **US-64 Cullasaja Gorge:** dead in the gorge itself; returns in Franklin. Franklin ↔ Bryson City (US-441/74): solid.
- **Road to Nowhere:** fades past the park boundary; nothing at the tunnel.
- **THE BIG ONE — Stecoah → NC-28 → Cable Cove → Fontana → Deals Gap → Cheoah Dam:** essentially a total dead zone ~2 hours long. Verizon is dead at Fontana Dam (tiny pocket near the marina only); AT&T has scattered pockets (~68% area coverage vs Verizon's 22%). Treat 3:30–5:45pm as radio silence.
- **Cherohala Skyway:** dead for all 43 mi, both states. **Robbinsville is your last radar check** — do it at the Shell.
- **Tellico River Rd (Bald River):** dead.
- **Foothills Parkway:** patchy; reliable signal returns at Look Rock towers and Walland.

## 6. DRIVE-WITH-CARE SECTIONS (ranked)

1. **US-64 Cullasaja Gorge (Highlands→Franklin), ~12:05–12:30pm — most dangerous road of the day.** Blade-cut into a cliff: no shoulder, low 1930s stone wall, blind curves, rubbernecking waterfall traffic. The **Cullasaja Falls pull-off is a tiny shoulder on a blind curve — skip it by default in a 7 Series** (the judge already flagged it as the day's riskiest parking). Run the gorge at the limit, lights on.
2. **US-129 from NC-28 junction (Deals Gap) → Cheoah Dam → Robbinsville, ~5:15–5:55pm — heaviest motorcycle exposure.** You clip the northern tip of the Dragon's territory; sport bikes cross the centerline on blind lefts. Monday after July 4 is quieter but never empty. Hold your lane, use pull-offs to release bikes stacking behind you.
3. **NC-28 "Hellbender" (Stecoah→Fontana):** blind crests + bike spillover; deer near the lake late afternoon.
4. **Cherohala Skyway:** the pavement is flawless but watch (a) fog banks above 4,500 ft appearing in minutes, (b) gravel/rock washed across curve apexes after any rain, (c) deer/bear/boar toward dusk, (d) 2–5pm thunderstorm cells — **decide at Robbinsville: if radar shows red over Monroe County, run it stop-free.**
5. **Cable Cove Rd (FR 520), ~2.4 mi gravel:** smooth, graded, sedan-safe at 15 mph — the only unpaved stretch of the day. After heavy rain expect a couple of soft spots; if it looks washboarded at the turn-in, it isn't worth a rim.
6. **Lakeview Drive:** pedestrians walking the roadway to the tunnel; 6.5 mi of glass — don't let the fresh pavement talk you into speed near the parking area.
7. **Foothills Parkway at dusk:** deer, heavy. Last 20 min of the day, highest animal-strike risk.

## 7. SCHEDULE REALITY CHECK

At honest mountain pace (~80% of Google) plus your stop list, a 9:30 departure lands the hotel ~9:15–9:30pm — **after** sunset but inside civil twilight, with the sunset itself spent at Look Rock (arguably better than arriving at 8:15 and watching sunset from a parking lot). Wheel time ≈ 8.4–8.7h; door-to-door ≈ 11.75h. **Bald River Falls (+35 min) and a daylight Look Rock are mutually exclusive from a 9:30 start — default: skip Bald River; it's the storm-day substitute.** To claw back ~30 min if you want a pre-dark hotel arrival: skip Fontana dam-top walk (visitor-center view only, −15), tunnel stop 20 min instead of 30 (−10), lunch 30 min (−10). Or just leave at 9:00.
