# Terminus Hotel Deep-Dive — Alcoa/Maryville, TN (night of Mon July 6, 2026)

Route terminus: US-321 → Alcoa, arriving ~7:45-8:15pm. All Alcoa candidates cluster within 2 miles of McGhee Tyson Airport, ~20-25 min (17 mi) north of Look Rock Overlook via the Foothills Parkway exit and US-321/Alcoa Hwy. Post-July-4th Monday is one of the softest booking nights of the summer here — night-of availability is very likely, but the downtown Maryville option (candidate #5) is the only one that could genuinely be tight.

## Ranked Candidates

### 1. Home2 Suites by Hilton Alcoa Knoxville Airport — the planned terminus, and correctly so
- **Google Maps:** "Home2 Suites by Hilton Alcoa Knoxville Airport", 1864 Pauling St, Alcoa, TN 37701 (~35.757, -83.986)
- **Age:** New-build, opened **2023** (TripAdvisor/Booking listings created 2023; OTA "welcoming guests since 2023"). Newest branded hotel in Blount County that's confirmably open.
- **Rate:** Kayak floor $123; typical July weeknight **$150-185**. Post-holiday Monday should land near the low end — inside budget.
- **Parking:** Free, flat **surface lot** ringing the building (new Springbrook Farm district, low-density, well lit) — easy visible spot for the white 7 Series; no garage.
- **Reviews:** Booking.com **9.0/10** (138+ reviews; facilities 9.6, comfort 9.5, cleanliness 9.3); TripAdvisor #3 of 18 in Alcoa. Consistent with a 4.4-4.6 Google profile for a 2023 Home2 — meets the 4.3+ bar comfortably.
- **Distance from route end:** 0 — it *is* the route end.
- **Standout:** All-suite rooms with kitchenette (nice after an 11-hour day), outdoor pool, Spin2Cycle laundry+gym. Dinner: 5-min drive to the Hunters Crossing/Hall Rd corridor (Aubrey's, Cheddar's, Chick-fil-A) or 10 min to downtown Maryville.

### 2. Fairfield Inn & Suites Knoxville Airport Alcoa — the Marriott play, literally next door
- **Google Maps:** "Fairfield Inn & Suites Knoxville Airport Alcoa", 1865 Pauling St, Alcoa, TN 37701 (directly across from #1)
- **Age:** New-build, opened **~2020-2021** (TripAdvisor listing d22259937 created 2021; still marketed as "Brand New Hotel" in Springbrook Farm Development). 87 rooms.
- **Rate:** Kayak floor $110-132; typical July weeknight **$135-175** — cheapest way to stay on-brand Marriott.
- **Parking:** Free surface lot, same open new-development setting as the Home2.
- **Reviews:** Booking.com **8.8/10**; strong recent cleanliness/staff comments. Likely ~4.3-4.4 Google — at the bar.
- **Distance:** 0 (shares the terminus block).
- **Standout:** Free airport shuttle, outdoor pool, Marriott points. Slightly older-feeling than the Home2 but same lot, same block. **This is the pre-identified backup: if one of the pair spikes in price, the other almost certainly hasn't.**

### 3. Hampton Inn Knoxville-Airport (Alcoa) — renovated value pick
- **Google Maps:** "Hampton Inn Knoxville-Airport", 148 International Ave, Alcoa, TN 37701
- **Age:** Older bones (1990s build) but **fully renovated 2019** (81 rooms); 2025-2026 reviews still call rooms "immaculate… all new fixtures."
- **Rate:** Kayak floor $96; typical July weeknight **$130-170** — likely the best sub-$150 shot.
- **Parking:** Free surface lot (standard suburban Hampton pad, directly across from the airport entrance).
- **Reviews:** Booking.com location 9.2/10; overall guest sentiment strong but with occasional maintenance/elevator gripes — call it borderline 4.2-4.4 Google. Verify score on Maps before committing.
- **Distance:** ~1 mile from the Pauling St terminus.
- **Standout:** Praised hot breakfast/waffles; closest walk to a cluster of chain restaurants on the Alcoa Hwy strip. Ranked below #1-2 only because it's a renovation, not a new build, and QC is less consistent.

### 4. Courtyard by Marriott Knoxville Airport Alcoa — full-service comfort, oldest of the set
- **Google Maps:** "Courtyard Knoxville Airport Alcoa", 141 Furrow Way, Alcoa, TN 37701
- **Age:** Built **2006, renovated 2015** (Meetings & Conventions venue data) — fails the "2019+" ideal unless a newer refresh has landed; treat as the compromise option.
- **Rate:** Kayak floor $104; typical July weeknight **$150-190**.
- **Parking:** Free surface lot (2-story building, big flat lot — actually one of the easiest lots of the group).
- **Reviews:** TripAdvisor ~4/5; solid but unremarkable. Probably ~4.2-4.3 Google.
- **Distance:** ~1.5 mi from terminus, near the Hunters Crossing retail area — **best walkable-dinner radius of the Alcoa cluster** (Aubrey's, several sit-down options within a few hundred yards).
- **Standout:** The Bistro serves dinner + beer/wine on site — the only Alcoa candidate where you can get a hot meal and a drink without re-starting the car after 8pm. If arrival slips past 8:30, that matters.

### 5. Holiday Inn Express & Suites Downtown Maryville — wildcard, VERIFY IT'S OPEN
- **Google Maps:** search "Holiday Inn Express Downtown Maryville TN" / 202 W Broadway Ave, Maryville, TN 37801
- **Age:** Ground-up new build; broke ground **March 2024**, ~$16M, 98 rooms, ~1-year build → projected open **late 2025/early 2026**. As of research date I could NOT confirm it has opened — no live TN booking listing found (searches keep resolving to Maryville, Missouri). **Check IHG app the morning of.**
- **Rate:** New downtown product, expect **$160-200** — top of budget.
- **Parking:** **Two-floor parking garage under the building — a direct violation of the free-surface-lot requirement.** This is why it ranks #5 despite being the newest and coolest.
- **Reviews:** Too new to have a track record.
- **Distance:** ~4 mi / 10 min from the Pauling St terminus (and actually *closer* to the US-321 approach — you'd pass through Maryville anyway).
- **Standout:** **Rooftop bar with foothills views** — the only rooftop in Blount County — plus genuinely walkable downtown Maryville dinner (Greenway, breweries, Foothills Milling Co.). If the driver values the rooftop + walkability over the surface-lot rule for one night, this is the fun pick.

### 6. Tru by Hilton Alcoa Knoxville Airport — cheap and new, but reviews miss the bar
- **Google Maps:** "Tru by Hilton Alcoa Knoxville Airport", 125 Furrow Way, Alcoa, TN 37701 (next to the Courtyard)
- **Age:** Built **2020**.
- **Rate:** Kayak floor $91; typical July weeknight **$110-145** — cheapest candidate.
- **Parking:** Free surface lot.
- **Reviews:** TripAdvisor 4/5 but only **#8 of 18 in Alcoa**, with repeated 2025-26 complaints about bathroom mold, bugs, and inconsistent service; Yelp ~4.1. **Likely below the 4.3 Google threshold** — include only as the budget fallback if everything else spikes.
- **Distance:** ~1.5 mi from terminus; same walkable Hunters Crossing dinner radius as the Courtyard.
- **Standout:** Price. Nothing else.

## Booking play for night-of
Check rates mid-afternoon (e.g., during the Fontana Dam stop — last reliable cell service before the Cherohala) via Hilton and Marriott apps: **Home2 first, Fairfield second**. Both under $160 post-holiday Monday is the expected outcome. If the Maryville downtown HIE shows as bookable and under $200, weigh the rooftop against the garage.

## Backup towns 30-45 min further along (westbound continuation)

**A. West Knoxville / Turkey Creek (Farragut)** — ~30-35 min from Alcoa via Pellissippi Pkwy (I-140 W → I-40/75). Deep modern-brand inventory that essentially never sells out on a Monday: **Tru by Hilton Knoxville West Turkey Creek** (380 Lovell Rd, new-build ~2019, surface lot), **Homewood Suites Knoxville West at Turkey Creek** (10935 Turkey Dr), plus Home2, Hampton, Courtyard and Aloft variants within 2 miles. Turkey Creek is a giant retail/restaurant district — dozens of dinner options, all free flat parking. Typical rates $120-170.

**B. Lenoir City** — ~35-40 min from Alcoa via US-321 W to I-75. **Fairfield Inn & Suites Knoxville Lenoir City/I-75** (Marriott, recent build, "brand new" per reviews, free surface lot, ~$120-150) is the anchor; positions the car directly on I-75/I-40 for any next-day direction (Chattanooga, Nashville, Cumberland Plateau). Quieter and cheaper than Turkey Creek, fewer dinner options.

Both backups sit *away* from the Smokies tourist funnel (Gatlinburg/Sevierville direction is the one direction to avoid — inventory there is resort-priced and crowd-adjacent even post-holiday).
